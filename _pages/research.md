I am currently working as a student under [Matt Baker](https://sites.google.com/view/mattbakermath/home/) at the Georgia Institute of Technology. My research is in algebraic combinatorics, especially matroid theory and its connections with tropical geometry, algebraic geometry, and pastures. My dissertation has the working title *Dressians of Matroids through Pastures*.

## Current projects

### Papers
- **Foundations of Elliptic Matroids**, with Matthew Baker and Noah Solomon. Manuscript in preparation.
- **Foundations of Matroids, Part 3**, with Matthew Baker and Oliver Lorscheid. Manuscript in preparation.
- **Tropical Point Configurations and Valuated Matroids**. Manuscript in preparation.

### Macaulay2
- **Pastures**, a Macaulay2 package, with Justin Chen and Tianyi Zhang.
- **Rigidity**, a Macaulay2 package, with Kalina Mincheva, Daniel Irving Bernstein, and additional collaborators, in preperation.

## Selected contribution

I computationally verified the conjectured reduced-Dressian behavior of the elliptic matroids \(T_n\) for \(7 \leq n \leq 26\). This computation is acknowledged in Baker–Huh–Kummer–Lorscheid, *Lorentzian Polynomials and Matroids over Triangular Hyperfields* (2025).
