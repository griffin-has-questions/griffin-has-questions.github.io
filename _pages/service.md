I care about building mathematical communities in which students can find support, take intellectual risks, and contribute to the culture around them. I've been involved with a lot of activities, and I list some references here.

## Georgia Tech

- **College of Sciences Graduate Student Committee, 2024–present:** With C-PIES, I help select speakers for the Making Science Accessible seminar series for interdisciplinary presentations across the College of Science. We also host a yearly Bowling and Poster Competition in the spring! 
- **Graduate Peer Mentor Coordinator, 2023–2026.** While I no longer run the Peer Mentor Program at Georgia Tech, I am in contact with the current organizers. If you are an incoming math graduate student at any university, I encourage you to reach out and see if there is a similar program in place.
- **High School Math Day.** I help write problems, proctor exams, and organize fun events for [High School Math Day](https://hsmd.math.gatech.edu) here at Georgia Tech. The last one took place on Saturday, March 7th, 2026. We expect registration to open in November 2026 and close in January 2027.
- **Emory Math Circle, 2022–2024.** I was an instructor at Emory Math Circle while they were accepting online Georgia Tech instructors. [Here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/10sOy-kqvxX2cF-qus-3A-DiJ3ReXv6lQ) are some online math circle materials I made. 
- **Struggling with School.** If you find yourself struggling, know that there is always help and support to be found. If you a are a Georgia Tech student, you can find mental health care [here](https://mentalhealth.gatech.edu/), tutoring and academic support [here](https://www.success.gatech.edu/tutoring/), and financial aid resources [here](https://finaid.gatech.edu/). I encourage everyone to discuss their struggles with their advisors and peers. You are not going through this alone.
