Rules & edge connections

Pieces slide until a wall or another piece stops them. Diagonal bumpers turn all pieces by 90° without stopping them. Moves ending on a bumper or looping forever are unavailable.

Dashed edges connect; matching edge numbers show which rows or columns meet. Solid outer edges are walls. Undo removes the last move from your attempt.
