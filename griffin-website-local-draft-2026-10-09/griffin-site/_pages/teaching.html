My teaching centers on active participation, collaborative problem solving, and feedback that helps students take ownership of their mathematical work. I am especially interested in undergraduate teaching and advising.

Current course · Fall 2026

MATH 1499: Support for Introductory Calculus

As instructor of record, I designed and teach a flipped, discussion-centered course for 15 students who placed beyond precalculus but benefit from additional work on foundational ideas.

  • Interactive readings before class
  • Collaborative worksheets and quizzes
  • Student board presentations and whole-class synthesis
  • Targeted review informed by an initial diagnostic
  • Mathematical materials revised for screen-reader accessibility
