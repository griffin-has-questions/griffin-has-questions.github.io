    <p class="eyebrow">Selected contribution</p>
    <h2 id="contribution-heading">Computing elliptic Dressians</h2>
    <p>I computationally verified the conjectured reduced-Dressian behavior of the elliptic matroids \(T_n\) for \(7 \leq n \leq 26\). This computation is acknowledged in Baker–Huh–Kummer–Lorscheid, <em>Lorentzian Polynomials and Matroids over Triangular Hyperfields</em> (2025).</p>
